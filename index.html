Why it isn’t working

People are visiting. They’re just not booking.

You post, you run ads, and people do click through. Then most of them close the tab. When a page isn’t bringing in bookings, it usually comes down to one of these four things.

  1. The offer isn’t clear

    Visitors should know what you offer and who it’s for within a few seconds. If they have to scroll to work it out, most won’t bother.

  2. The ad and page don’t match

    Someone clicks an ad about a free class and lands on your homepage. They came for one thing, can’t find it, and leave.

  3. It’s hard to use on a phone

    Most people will see your page on a phone. Tiny text, slow loading or a fiddly form is enough to lose them.

  4. Nobody follows up

    Someone fills in your form, then hears nothing for days. By the time you reply, they’ve booked with someone else.

How I fix it

I map the steps first, then build the pages.

Before I design anything, I work out where your visitors come from, what they need to hear and what you want them to do. Every page is then built around that.

  1. A clear offer

    What you do, who it’s for and why you, in the words your clients actually use.

  2. The full route, mapped

    Opt-in, sales page, booking, thank-you page and follow-up, planned before anything is designed.

  3. Built for phones first

    Designed for the screen most people use, then built in GoHighLevel, Systeme or as a custom site.

  4. One next step per page

    Each page asks for one thing: book a call, buy, or leave your details.
